## The tier wall

Each item declares an evidence tier, and the tier caps what its result may ever be cited for. Results travel from measurement validity to diagnostic to primary in one direction only: a diagnostic finding that the instrument is sensitive to some feature is a fact about the instrument, and does not become evidence for the hypothesis because it appeared in the same table. `lab.spec.cite()` raises rather than relying on anyone remembering this.

| Tier | May support | Items |
|---|---|---|
| `MEASUREMENT_VALIDITY` | instrument_validity | C01, C02 |
| `DIAGNOSTIC` | instrument_validity, explanation_elimination | D01, D02, D03, D04, D05, U01, U02, U03, U04, N01, N02, N03, N04 |
| `PRIMARY` | instrument_validity, explanation_elimination, mechanism_effect | L01, L02, L03, L04, L05, L06, R01, R02, R03, R04 |

**Gate:** C01, C02. A failure there halts interpretation of the run rather than lowering a number in it.
